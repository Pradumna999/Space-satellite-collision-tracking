# Model and data limitations

1. **Public GP elements (TLE/OMM)** are SGP4 mean elements with undocumented, object-dependent errors (typically ~1 km at epoch, several km/day growth). Encounters from them are screening results only; they never get a Pc.
2. **No covariance propagation.** Pc is available only where covariances are supplied at TCA (CDM re-assessment). OEM covariances at other epochs are not propagated.
3. **2D short-term Pc assumptions:** rectilinear relative motion, static Gaussian covariance, uncorrelated errors, spherical objects with analyst-supplied HBR. Low-speed or long-duration encounters are flagged but not handled by a 3D method (none implemented).
4. **No comparison against NASA CARA** or any operational tool has been made (not accessible in this build).
5. **Scenario model** is two-body + J2 with the pole along GCRF z; it departs from SGP4/ephemerides by hundreds of metres to kilometres over hours (reported per scenario). Absolute scenario miss distances are model-dependent; compare arms. Uncertainty is not propagated; broader consequences are only checked against objects in the same dataset.
6. **Earth orientation:** bundled IERS EOP data end 2026-08-28 in this build; later ITRF conversions assume UT1−UTC = 0 (≤ ~0.5 km in LEO, Earth-fixed only). The leap-second table is valid to 2027-06-28.
7. **Visualisation:** orbit lines join propagated samples (visual interpolation). The Earth texture ignores precession-nutation (~0.4°). No uncertainty volumes are drawn in 3D.
8. **Screening completeness** holds while propagators are valid at grid instants. Objects with SGP4 errors or ephemeris gaps are invisible there (reported as warnings).
9. **Scale:** memory grows with objects × grid steps. Full-catalogue runs (~30k objects) have not been performed.
10. **ML:** synthetic labels from an assumed uncertainty model; no real-world validity.
11. **Synthetic demo data** is generated by ASTRAL and labelled throughout. It must not be mixed with or mistaken for real observations.
12. **CCSDS conformance** was checked against the parsers' own validators only; editions are unverified (DATA_FORMATS.md).
